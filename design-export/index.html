The heritage

Made in Emsdetten.

Every markilux system is vertically integrated. The fabric is woven in our own mill, the frames extruded in our facility, the motors and bionic tendon arms engineered in our research laboratory. The result is a level of precision and consistency no assembled product can replicate: the quiet signature of well-considered homes for five decades.

Market context

Where this sits in the category.

Retractable outdoor shading is offered across three distinct tiers in the American market. The differences are not subtle. Below is the honest landscape.

Entry awnings

$3–6K installed

Typically US-assembled from imported components. Woven acrylic fabric, lateral arm construction without integrated tendon arms. Three-to-ten-year functional life.

Premium systems

$6–9K installed

US-manufactured with European motor components. Sunbrella fabric upgrades and heavier-gauge frames with optional semi-cassette protection. Ten-to-fifteen-year service life.

European architectural

$7–15K installed

Vertically integrated European manufacture with manufacturer-owned fabric mills and engineered bionic tendon arms. Twenty-to-thirty-year life, specified by architects.
